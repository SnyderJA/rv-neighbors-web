Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026 · RV Neighbors mobile app

RV Neighbors helps RV campers find and connect with other campers nearby. Doing that means handling your location, which is the most sensitive thing we hold. This policy explains exactly what we collect, what other people can see, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. We never show anyone your exact position. Other users see a pin deliberately offset from where you really are, by at least a mile. We do not sell your personal information, and the app does not currently show advertising.

How your location is handled

This is the part worth reading carefully.

Nobody sees where you actually are

When another user sees you on the map, they are not seeing your position. They see a point offset from it in a random direction, by at least one mile and up to a distance based on the visibility radius you chose. The distance shown alongside that pin is the distance to the offset point, not to you. The offset is consistent for each person viewing you, so it cannot be averaged away by looking repeatedly.

You control who can see you at all

In Settings → Privacy you can switch location sharing off entirely, choose separately whether connections, close friends and other campers can see you, and set a different visibility radius for each. Stealth mode hides you from everyone immediately.

Stealth mode hides you from other users. It does not stop the app recording your position. Your device keeps sending its location while the map is open, so that when you switch stealth off you reappear where you actually are rather than where you were hours ago. We simply stop showing it to anybody.

If you do not want your location recorded at all, switch location sharing off and deny the app location permission in your phone's settings.

Only while the app is open

We collect location only while the app is in the foreground and you are on the map. The app does not track you in the background, and does not ask for background location permission.

What other users can see

  • Your name, display name, photos, RV details and interests
  • An offset position on the map, if you have allowed it for that audience
  • An approximate distance, calculated to the offset point
  • Whether you are connected to them, or have a request pending

Your email address is never shown to other users.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers. The app does not currently show advertising. If we introduce it, this page will say exactly what is collected and who receives it, before any of it happens.

Your location is treated differently from everything else. We do not sell or share your precise location with advertisers, and we have no plans to. Other users only ever see the offset position described above.

Notifications

If you allow notifications we store a token identifying your device, so we can tell you about connection requests and other activity. You can turn individual notification types off in Settings → Notifications, or all of them in your phone's settings. Signing out removes the token from your device's record.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, location, connections, settings and notification token. It cannot be undone, and we do not keep a copy.

How long we keep things

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it. Routine server logs, which may contain an IP address, are retained by our hosting provider for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

Children

RV Neighbors is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and change the date at the top. We will announce material changes in the app before they take effect — particularly anything involving advertising or a new company receiving your data — so that you can decide whether to carry on using RV Neighbors or delete your account.
