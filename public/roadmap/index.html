RV Neighbors · Product roadmap

The road from beta to 3.0 and beyond

RV Neighbors helps RVers see who's camped nearby, connect, message, and stay in control of how precisely they're found. This is the plan, build by build: what each one adds and the lines we won't cross.

One headline feature per buildEach build adds one core feature plus a few fixes, so releases stay small and dependable.
Scope locks before testingNew bugs found while testing go to the next build, unless this build caused them.
Verified before it shipsEvery build is checked on real iPhones and Android phones before testers get it.
Quality over datesNo calendar promises. A build ships when it's ready.
Leg 1

Road to the public launch

Safety, privacy and the features a full audience needs, ending in the 3.0 release.

  1. Build19
    Shipped

    Smoother typing, sturdier foundation

    Keyboard fixes across chat, sign-in, sign-up and the report sheet on both phones; full-screen neighbor list from the map; Accept and Decline on profiles; real build numbers on Android; behind-the-scenes reliability and security work.

  2. Build20
    Up next

    Reports reach a real person, fast

    When someone files a report, the team is emailed right away, the reporter gets a confirmation, and once a person has reviewed it the reporter hears back. App stores expect reports to be acted on within 24 hours; this closes that loop.

    First version
    • Instant alert to the team when a report comes in
    • "We got your report" email to the reporter
    • Reviewed in the admin console, then a "a person reviewed this and is taking action" email
    • A daily check-in that tells us which app version each phone runs

    Never tells the reporter what action was taken against the other person.

  3. Build21
    Planned

    Location privacy, rebuilt

    A clearer proximity screen. Instead of picking distances by hand, choose close, narrow or wide, and control separately who can see you and how precisely.

    • Three simple presets
    • Separate controls for who sees you and how exact your location is
    • Privacy hardening

    Never shows anyone's location more precisely than a five-mile radius. Not for strangers, not for close friends.

  4. Build22
    Planned

    Know when friends are nearby, without opening the app

    Your location updates while you travel, and you get a gentle nudge when one of your connections is in range.

    • Watches for big moves in a battery-friendly way; your map spot moves only after two checks in a row put you 50+ miles from where you were
    • Nudges only about connections who aren't in stealth mode, within the radius you choose
    • At most 5 nudges a day, only during daytime hours
    • One switch turns background location off, and the rest of the app still works

    A nudge never says who is nearby or anything about them. Background location is never forced on.

  5. Build23
    Planned

    Creator partnerships and a cleanup pass

    A partnership page for RV YouTube creators, showing what the app does today and where it's going, with a partner application. Plus a round of fixes and messaging safety improvements.

    • Creator page with a pitch video and partner application
    • Spam protection and tidy-up rules for messages
    • Announcements from the RV Neighbors team
  6. Build24
    Planned

    Group messaging

    Caravans and campfire crews can coordinate in one thread instead of a separate group chat.

    • Groups of up to 25, made from people you're already connected with
    • A group name; the creator adds and removes members
    • Anyone can leave; the group ends when its creator leaves

    Being in a group never connects you to the other members or opens private messages with them.

  7. Build25
    Planned

    Free and paid plans

    A free tier and paid subscriptions, with the first ads for free users. Details are still being worked out.

    An ad is never dressed up as a real RVer on the map.

  8. Build26
    Milestone

    Public launch, version 3.0

    Everything from Builds 20 to 25 goes public after a full test pass on every flow. Our beta testers become founding members.

Leg 2

Growing the community

Ways to show where you've been, prove you're who you say, and gather around shared interests.

  1. Build27
    Planned

    National Park Passport

    Every national park gets its own page and custom badge. Mark a park visited, add a photo and a short story, and note whether its RV park is worth a stay. Your visited parks show on your profile as badges, a natural conversation starter.

  2. Build28
    Planned

    Verified members and profile badges

    An optional ID check through a trusted verification partner earns a Verified badge, so people know they're dealing with a real person. We keep only the result, never a copy of your ID.

    • Trust badges: Verified, Verified RV Tech, Founder, Creator partner
    • Achievement badges earned by what you do in the app, such as visiting all 63 national parks

    A fun achievement badge never looks like a trust badge.

  3. Build29
    Planned

    Hardening and fixes

    A build dedicated to reliability and polish.

  4. Builds30–31
    Planned · two builds

    Communities

    Verified hosts, such as creators and trusted organizers, run their own invite-only communities, Facebook-group style.

    Build 30Verified host accounts and invite-only communities, moderated by their hosts
    Build 31A curated feed from RV Neighbors, vetted sources and creator partners

    Posts never reveal anyone's location unless they choose to say it. Only ID-verified hosts can run a community.

Leg 3

Help on the road

Two big features, each rolled out over several builds so every step is solid before the next.

  1. Builds32–35
    Planned · four builds

    Call for Help

    When you're stuck roadside, send a help request to RVers who can actually reach you.

    Build 32Ask your connections for help; your pin glows on their map for an hour
    Build 33Reach verified RVers within 50 miles, with daily limits
    Build 34"I can help" responses and marking it resolved
    Build 35Help categories and smarter matching

    Not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.

  2. Builds36–39
    Planned · four builds

    RV Tech Locator

    Find trustworthy RV techs and repair shops wherever you are.

    Build 36A curated directory with tap-to-call
    Build 37Ratings, reviews and suggestions from RVers
    Build 38Tech accounts with a Verified RV Tech badge
    Build 39Request service in the app
  3. Build40
    Planned

    Stabilization

    Polish and reliability across everything above.

Plans can change as we learn from testers. Order and scope are reviewed before each build starts.
