Installing it

  1. Open the email on your Android phone

    Tap Download the app. Your browser saves a .zip file.

  2. Unzip it

    Open your Files app, go to Downloads and tap the .zip. Android extracts it and you get rv-neighbors-beta.apk — that's the app itself.

  3. Allow your Files app to install it

    Tap the extracted .apk. Android will stop you the first time, with something like:

    For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source.

    Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back and tap the file again. You only do this once.

  4. Tap Install

    Google may offer to scan the app first, or warn that it doesn't recognise the developer. That's expected for an app that isn't on the Play Store yet — choose Install anyway or More details → Install anyway.

  5. Open it and create an account

    Tap Join Your Neighbors on the first screen. You'll get an email to confirm your address.

Is this safe to install?

It's our own app, signed by us, and it's the same build that will go to the Play Store. The warnings you'll see are Android being cautious about anything that didn't come from the Play Store — they're about where the file came from, not what's in it. If you'd rather wait for the Play Store version, that's completely fine; tell us and we'll let you know when it's there.

Why is it a zip instead of the app?

Chrome on Android currently has a bug where app files never finish downloading — the progress bar sits at 100% forever and the file never appears. Putting the app in a zip avoids it entirely. It costs you one extra tap to extract, and it's the difference between the download working and not.

My download is stuck at 100%

That's the Chrome bug above, and it means you downloaded the app file directly rather than the zip. Cancel it, open your link again, and you should get a .zip. If you're still getting an app file, email us.

Why not just use the Play Store?

Google requires us to finish an identity verification before we can publish, and that's with them at the moment. Rather than hold up testing, we're sending the app directly.

What does "beta" mean here?

The app works, but you're seeing it before everyone else, so there will be rough edges. If something looks wrong, that's worth telling us about — that's the point of you being here.

How do I get the next version?

Tap your original link again and install over the top. Your account and settings stay as they are. We'll email you when there's something new.
