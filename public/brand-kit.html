RV Neighbors is a community platform for the RV lifestyle — connecting travelers parked near each other, enabling families to travel together, and building a network of fellow road-dwellers. The brand is warm, outdoorsy, and grounded: deep forest greens meet golden amber, like a campfire at dusk under the pines.

01 — Identity

The Mark

The RVN monogram sets a map pin as the V — the product is about finding people near you. The full lockup is the default; the monogram alone is for square and small contexts where the wordmark would be illegible.

rvn-logo-full.svg · on light
rvn-logo-full-reversed.svg · on dark
rvn-mark.svg · monogram
rvn-mark-mono.svg · single colour

Use the lockup

Wherever there is room for the wordmark — site headers, splash, documents.

Use the monogram

App icons, favicons, avatars, anything square or under ~120px wide.

Use single colour

On amber or any brand-colour ground, where the two-tone version would lose its amber half.

Logo colours differ from the UI palette. The delivered artwork uses #2F5945 and #DA9902, which are not tokens in the app. The reversed and single-colour variants swap the green for #F5F5F0 so the mark holds up on dark and amber grounds. Reconciling the artwork with the UI palette is an open decision.

04 — Voice

Brand Voice

Nature-Rooted

Forest greens and earth tones ground the palette in the outdoors. Every colour choice should evoke open land and fresh air.

Warm & Welcoming

Amber accents bring the warmth of a campfire — approachable, human, and community-first in every interaction.

On the Road

Language and metaphor borrow from RV culture. We speak the language of travelers — casual, direct, and always moving forward.

SayNeighbors, crew, rig, on the road, parked nearby, check in
AvoidUsers, contacts, vehicles, geolocation, engagement
ToneCasual and direct. Short sentences. Never corporate, never cute.
