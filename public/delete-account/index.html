Delete your account

Last updated [RELEASE DATE] · RV Neighbors mobile app

You can delete your RV Neighbors account at any time, from inside the app or by asking us.

In the app

  1. Open RV Neighbors and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can no longer use the app, email support@rvneighborsapp.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm the request before we act on it.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photos, location, connections, the messages you sent, settings, notification token and device details. Messages other people sent you stay in their conversations. It cannot be undone.

A few things are kept for safety: reports you made or that were made about you, with the content and photos copied when they were made; messages you sent to our support team, with your name and email, so we can handle your request; and error reports already sent from your phone, with your account no longer attached. The Privacy Policy explains what is kept and why.

Contact

Questions about deleting your data: support@rvneighborsapp.com
