You're invited to try RV Neighbors

It takes about two minutes. You'll need an iPhone.

Open this page on your iPhone

The beta installs on the phone itself, so this page has to be open there. Scan this with your iPhone camera:

Or type this into Safari on your iPhone:

rvneighborsapp.com/testflight

  1. Install TestFlight

    Apple's free app for trying apps before they're in the App Store. RV Neighbors installs through it.

    Get TestFlight

  2. Come back and tap Join

    This opens TestFlight and asks you to accept the invitation. Tap Accept.

    Join the beta

  3. Tap Install

    RV Neighbors lands on your home screen like any other app. The icon is dark green with a gold pin.

  4. Open it and create an account

    Tap Join Your Neighbors on the first screen. You'll get an email to confirm your address.

Is TestFlight safe?

It's made by Apple and it's how every iPhone app gets tested before release. It's free, and it doesn't ask for payment details.

What does "beta" mean here?

The app works, but you're seeing it before everyone else, so there will be rough edges. If something looks wrong, that's worth telling us about — that's the point of you being here.

The link says the beta is full

There are 100 places and they're first come, first served. Email us and we'll sort it out.

How do I get updates?

TestFlight tells you when there's a new version. Open TestFlight and tap Update — you won't need this page again.
