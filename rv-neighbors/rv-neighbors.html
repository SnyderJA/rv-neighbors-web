Features

Built for Life on the Road

Everything you need to find your people, share the best spots, and stay connected across thousands of miles.

Radar Map

See fellow RVers on a live topographic map. Filter by distance — 5, 10, 25, or 50 miles — to find neighbors wherever you park.

Your Crew

Build a trusted inner circle. See where your close connections are, ping them when you roll into their area, and plan meetups effortlessly.

Real-Time Messaging

Direct messages, group chats, and location sharing — stay in sync with your crew without leaving the app. Plan campfire meetups, share boondocking spots, and coordinate caravans.

Rig Profiles

Showcase your setup — rig type, solar capacity, Starlink, length. Instantly see compatibility with potential crew before you even say hello.

Interest Matching

Filter nearby nomads by shared interests — hiking, remote work, kayaking, full-timing. Meet people you'll actually click with, not just random passersby.

Simple Setup

On the Road in 3 Steps

No complicated setup. Sign up, drop your pin, and start connecting the moment we launch.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up and build your profile in minutes. Add your rig details, travel style, and interests so the right people find you first.

2

Drop Your Pin

Share your current location to appear on the map. See everyone nearby in real time and filter by distance to find your ideal neighbors.

3

Build Your Crew

Send a message, add someone to your crew, or start a group chat. Turn "who else is out here?" into real community wherever you park.
